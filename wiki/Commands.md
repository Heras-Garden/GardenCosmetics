# Commands

- `/donate`
- `/cosmetics status`
- `/cosmetics donor on|off`
- `/cosmetics color #RRGGBB|reset`
- `/cosmetics font <font>|reset`
- `/cosmetics particles ...`
- `/cosmetics grant <player> <entitlement> <transaction-ref>`
- `/cosmetics revoke <player> <entitlement> <transaction-ref>`

Grant and revoke commands are intended for administrators and Tebex fulfillment.
